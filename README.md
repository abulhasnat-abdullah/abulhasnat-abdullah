---

I'm a junior Mechanical Engineering student at BUET, interested in robotics and automation. I like building autonomous robots: field robots, their digital twins, and the work of getting complex software stacks running on real hardware.

Most of my work is **integration**. I don't write SLAM or autonomy algorithms from scratch. I take open-source packages, configure and connect them, and make them work reliably on actual robots. Outside coursework, I lead the software sub-team of **Team Interplanetar**, BUET's Mars rover team.

Away from engineering, I paint as *Aquarelle Verse* and design visuals for events.

### Research interests

- **Sensor fusion and localization**
- **Uncertainty analysis**, including the mathematical modeling of uncertainty
- **Multi-agent, multi-sensor fusion**, a likely direction for my thesis
